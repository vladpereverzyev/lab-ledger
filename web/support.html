Lab Ledger Dental Support

Help for the desktop app for dental labs

Lab Ledger Dental is made by Vladyslav Pereverzyev. If something does not work as it should, or you have a question or an idea, these are the ways to get in touch.

Report a problem or ask a question

Open an issue on GitHub. Say which version you use (it is shown in Settings and in the bottom right corner of the window), which system it runs on and what happened. Please do not attach real patient data or a backup of your archive.

Write by email

For anything you would rather not post in public, write to info@vladpereverzyev.com.

Before you write

  • A forgotten administrator password is reset with the recovery code, from the sign-in screen. An administrator can read the code in Catalog, Settings.
  • Your data stays on your computer. Use Backup or Encrypted backup to keep a copy and Import backup to restore it on another computer.
  • The app works without an internet connection.

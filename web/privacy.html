Lab Ledger Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Lab Ledger is a desktop app for dental labs, made by Vladyslav Pereverzyev. This page explains what happens to the data you put into it. In short: it stays on your computer. I do not collect it, I do not see it and nobody else gets it through the app.

What the app stores

Everything you enter in Lab Ledger is saved in a file on your own computer: works, patients, clients, shipping, materials, costs, taxes, lab details and the users of the app. The app shows where this file is under Settings.

  • There is no account to create and no server to sign in to.
  • Passwords of the app users are never saved as text. Only a salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hash is kept, in the same local file.
  • Backups, Excel exports and the automatic Excel copy are written only where you choose to save them.

What the app sends

Lab Ledger has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no crash reports. Your data is never uploaded anywhere.

  • Microsoft Store and Mac App Store versions: the app makes no network requests of its own. Updates come through the store.
  • Version downloaded from GitHub: if the update check is switched on (you can turn it off in Settings), the app asks GitHub at most once a day which is the latest version. That request contains only the app name and version number. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any visit to a website, and handles it under the GitHub Privacy Statement. An update is downloaded only when you press the button.
  • Links in the app, such as the licence or the author's website, open in your browser only when you click them.

Patient data

Lab Ledger can hold personal data about patients and clients. Since it never leaves your computer, the lab that uses the app remains the controller of that data and is responsible for keeping it as the law requires, for example under the GDPR. Protect the computer and its backups as you would any other patient record, and give each person in the lab their own user with only the permissions they need.

Browser demo

The online demo runs entirely in your browser with sample data. Anything you change there is kept only in your browser's storage. The page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visits under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Lab Ledger is a work tool for dental labs and is not meant for children.

Changes

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new date at the top.

Contact
